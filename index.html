MOSAIC Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

MOSAIC is an educational and laboratory application designed to communicate with compatible optical measurement hardware.

Information We Collect

MOSAIC does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Hardware Communication and Measurement Data

The app may communicate with compatible MOSAIC hardware over local wireless connections, including Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, for measurement, device control, and data visualization purposes.

Measurement data generated during use is processed for operation of the app and is not transmitted to external servers unless explicitly initiated by the user through another service.

Tracking and Advertising

MOSAIC does not use third-party advertising, behavioral tracking, or advertising identifiers.

Device Permissions

The app may request access to network, Bluetooth, or local device permissions when required to connect to compatible measurement hardware. These permissions are used only to provide the requested hardware communication functionality.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If future versions of MOSAIC introduce additional data collection or cloud-based services, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the MOSAIC application, please visit: https://wp.optics.arizona.edu/jkoshel/
